Getting Started

Three simple steps
to a real connection

01

Create Your Profile

Choose a username, enter your age for age-aware matching, and select 5–10 genuine interests. No real name needed. No photo required. Your identity is yours to protect.

02

Match on Shared Interests

Luneya finds someone in your age range who shares at least three of your interests. No browsing strangers. No swiping. A thoughtful match — not a feed.

03

Build One Real Conversation

You get one active chat. Put your whole self into it. If it clicks, you can choose to share how to stay in touch. If not, you can end it and match again. No pressure.

Why Luneya Exists

Teens today are more connected — and lonelier than ever

The average teenager sends hundreds of messages a day. They have thousands of followers. They're constantly online. And yet study after study shows Gen Z feels deeply disconnected from real relationships.

Mainstream social media optimises for engagement — likes, reactions, virality. It rewards performance over presence. It replaces depth with volume.

Luneya is the opposite. It's designed to create the conditions for one genuine conversation — and get out of the way.

Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Luneya, safety, and how we protect your privacy.

Luneya is for ages 13–18. We use age verification during signup and age-aware matching to ensure teens only chat with others within their age band. Users under 16 require parental consent (COPPA compliant).

Messages are encrypted end-to-end. Your data is never sold to third parties. We're fully COPPA compliant and never use your information for advertising. You can delete your account anytime, and all your data is permanently removed.

Guardians receive a weekly digest with insights like activity trends, mood signals, and safety alerts — but never read actual messages. We believe transparency about the system matters more than surveillance of your teen.

Real humans review every report within 24 hours. We have clear consequences: warnings, temporary restrictions, or account suspension based on severity. Serious violations (threats, exploitation) are handled immediately and reported to appropriate authorities.

Yes. Core features (1-on-1 chat, age verification, safety) will always be free. We may add optional premium features like themes or export history, but we'll never paywall safety or core conversation features.

There's a report button on every message and profile. You can report abuse, inappropriate content, or safety concerns anonymously. Our team investigates immediately, and we follow up with real actions.
