Community Rules

A healthier internet starts with how we treat each other.

Luneya was built around meaningful conversations and genuine connections. These rules exist to help create an environment where everyone can feel comfortable, respected, and safe.

Be Kind

Treat other people with respect.

favorite Communicate respectfully.
forum Disagree without hostility.
groups Treat others as real people.
gpp_bad Harassment, bullying, threats and hate speech are prohibited.

Respect Boundaries

Everyone has different comfort levels and personal boundaries.

handshake Respect communication preferences.
do_not_disturb_on Respect requests to stop conversations.
warning No pressure, manipulation, or guilt-tripping.

Keep It Appropriate

Luneya is designed for teenagers and conversations should remain appropriate.

shield Keep conversations respectful.
visibility_off Explicit sexual content is not allowed.
image_not_supported Sharing sexual images is prohibited.

Be Honest

Authentic conversations begin with honesty.

verified_user Be truthful about who you are.
person_off Do not impersonate others.
badge Do not pretend to represent Luneya.

Protect Privacy

Respect both your privacy and the privacy of others.

lock Never share passwords.
home Do not expose addresses or personal information.
privacy_tip Respect private conversations.

No Spam Or Manipulation

Conversations should feel genuine, not transactional.

cancel No spam or mass messaging.
gpp_bad No scams or phishing attempts.
campaign No unsolicited promotions.

Reporting & Enforcement

Reports help us keep Luneya safer for everyone.

flag Report behaviour that violates these rules.
fact_check Reports are reviewed carefully.
gavel Violations may result in warnings, suspensions, or account removal.

“The goal isn't to punish people. The goal is to create a better place to talk.”

Treat people well. Respect boundaries. Help build the kind of community you'd want to be part of.
