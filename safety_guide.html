Safety Guide

Staying safe online shouldn't be complicated.

Luneya is designed to encourage thoughtful conversations and meaningful connections. While we work hard to create a safer environment, no platform can replace good judgement. These guidelines can help you protect your privacy and have a safer experience online.

Protect Your Privacy

Your personal information belongs to you.

lock Never share your password.
home Don't share your address.
school Avoid sharing school information.
badge Be careful with personal details that could identify you.

Take Things Slowly

Meaningful conversations take time. Trust should too.

schedule Take your time getting to know people.
psychology Not everyone online is who they claim to be.
groups Healthy friendships grow gradually.

Moving Conversations Elsewhere

Think carefully before sharing social media accounts or contact details.

alternate_email Never feel pressured to share social handles.
warning Be cautious when moving off-platform.
sentiment_satisfied Go at your own pace.

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, uncomfortable, or suspicious, trust that feeling.

block You can leave a conversation at any time.
report Report unsafe behaviour.
support Talk to a trusted adult if needed.

Reporting Problems

Reporting helps keep Luneya safer for everyone.

flag Report harassment, spam, scams, or inappropriate behaviour.
visibility Reports are reviewed carefully.
shield Safety is everyone's responsibility.

“The internet works best when people look out for each other.”

Stay curious. Stay kind. Stay safe.
