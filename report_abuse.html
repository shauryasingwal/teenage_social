Report Abuse

Something doesn't feel right?

If someone is making you uncomfortable, violating our rules, or behaving in a way that feels unsafe, you can report them. Reports help us keep Luneya safer for everyone.

What Should Be Reported?

If something feels wrong, it's okay to report it.

gpp_bad
Harassment
Unwanted, aggressive, or repeated harmful behaviour.
sentiment_very_dissatisfied
Bullying
Insults, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
mail
Spam
Repeated unwanted messages or promotions.
person_off
Impersonation
Pretending to be another person.
warning
Inappropriate Content
Content that violates Luneya's community rules.
paid
Scam or Fraud
Attempts to deceive or manipulate users.
help
Other
Anything that doesn't fit the categories above.

What Happens After A Report?

Every report goes through a review process.

flag
1. Report Submitted
Your report is securely received.
fact_check
2. Review
Reports are reviewed and investigated.
gavel
3. Action
Warnings, restrictions, suspensions, or bans may be applied.

Need Immediate Help?

Luneya cannot provide emergency services.

emergency If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
support Reach out to a trusted parent, guardian, teacher, or responsible adult.

Questions About Moderation?

If you have concerns about reports, moderation, or account actions, you can contact our Trust & Safety team.

mail trust@luneya.app

“Reporting isn't about getting someone in trouble.”
